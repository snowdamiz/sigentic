Savorly

For help with the Savorly apps or savorly.cooking, email hello@sigenticlabs.com with “Savorly” in the subject. Include the device you’re using and, if it’s about a specific recipe, a link to it.

Morse

Morse is in development. To ask about early builds or the waitlist, or to leave the waitlist, email hello@sigenticlabs.com. Bug reports for the desktop builds are welcome as GitHub issues. Please report security problems privately, as described on our security page.

Mesh

The Mesh documentation covers installation, the language and the toolchain. Report bugs and request features in GitHub issues.

Incant

Incant is in development and has no public release yet. Follow along and report problems in GitHub issues.

Your data & account deletion

  • Savorly: delete your account and its data from the app, or follow the steps on savorly.cooking/account-deletion.
  • Morse waitlist: email us and we’ll remove your address.
  • Anything else we hold about you: email hello@sigenticlabs.com to access, correct or delete it. See our privacy policy for details.
