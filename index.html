What we care about

  • Private by default.

    We collect the minimum, encrypt what we can, and never sell your data.

  • One job, done well.

    If a feature doesn’t earn its place, it doesn’t ship.

  • Respect for attention.

    No dark patterns, no infinite feeds, no guilt-trip notifications.

  • Made for everyone.

    Screen readers, dynamic type, contrast and reduced motion, from day one.

  • Built to last.

    Honest business models, so the software you rely on is still here years from now.
